Kaise kaam karta hai?

Sirf 3 simple steps

1

Donor post karta hai

Khane ki photo, quantity, pickup address aur expiry time ke saath donation request daalein.

2

NGO accept karta hai

Verified NGOs ko turant notification milta hai. Pehle accept karne wale ko donation mil jaati hai.

3

Pickup aur delivery

Picked Up → Delivered tak har step ka live tracking aur email update milta hai.
